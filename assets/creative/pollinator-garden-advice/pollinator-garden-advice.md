## Web text

There is tremendous enthusiasm for pollinator gardening, but what is best practice?

Research in Progress: "A Critical Review of Pollinator Garden Advice", presented at the Ecological Society of America Meeting in Salt Lake City, Utah, 28 July 2026.
Despite the immense popularity of pollinator gardening, it remains unclear whether common advice is evidence-based.
We therefore critically evaluated the "advice landscape" encountered by pollinator gardeners.
We first conducted a series of internet searches to collate advice encountered by the average gardener interested in supporting pollinators (n = 101 lists of guidelines).
We then reviewed the scientific literature to evaluate the underlying logic and ecological evidence in support of this advice.
What we find is that pollinator garden guidelines rarely cite empirical sources, and the ecological-logic underlying advice is not always articulated.
At the same time, many recommendations do align with scientific evidence, but with important nuance.

In this "Experimental Ecology Poster" we highlight some of the most common pollinator garden advice using this framework.
