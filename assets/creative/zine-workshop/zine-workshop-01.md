## Web text

During our weekly research group meeting, we all decided it would be a good idea to "think outside the doc" when it comes to our various ecological research projects.
We foraged for a wide range of physical source material and then using the classic "mini-zine" format, we got to work.

What is interesting, is that this was by far the most quiet research group meeting we have had in 10 years.
Why, you might ask?
Perhaps that by giving a bunch of ecologists permission to playfully and creatively explore and engage with their research, new ideas emerge, different connections are made.
And there need not be a sacrifice to the scientific rigor.

At the end of our group meeting, we all agreed that we should do this regularly...
