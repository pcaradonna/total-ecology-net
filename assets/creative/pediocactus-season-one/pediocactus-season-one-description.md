# Mountain Ball Cactus Demography Society

## Web text

Many have asked: "What is the question?" 
And for this project, the answer is: "What can mountain ball cactus teach us about being ecologists?"

Pediocactus simpsonii is a spherical ball shaped cactus. An adult is about the size of a grape fruit. 
It can withstand harsh, freezing winters in the mountains, and it persists up to about 10,000ft.
From a demography perspective, Pediocactus simpsonii is a lovely companion: easy to demarcate individuals and easy to measure their size...

...size is measured in the field as cactus diameter (d). Given a spherical plant, volume (v) = (pi/6) * d^3

The official gear of the Mountain Ball Cactus Demography Society. 

And! All of our demography data from the first season of field work are open access on the back of the sweatshirt!
