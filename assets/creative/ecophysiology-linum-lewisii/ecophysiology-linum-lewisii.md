# Ecophysiology of Linum lewisii

## Web text

The intersection of ecology, environment, and physiology in the context of climate change, explored through a massive snow melt and drought experiment with the montane wildflower, Linum lewisii.

Research, design, and printing conducted by Total Ecology humans in Chicago in honor of a dissertation defense.
