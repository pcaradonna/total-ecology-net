# Art-Science Exchange

## General Text

The Art/Science Exchange is a long-term artist residency project led by Paul CaraDonna that fosters genuine, bidirectional sharing among scientists and artists. Since 2013, we have been exploring how the confluence of art and science can broaden our understanding and engagement with nature. The residency mostly takes place at the Rocky Mountain Biological Laboratory, a remote, high elevation field station in Western Colorado (but it has now branched into new ecosystems, like the Sky Islands of Arizona).

The Art/Science Exchange has been supported by the National Science Foundation (Awards: DEB-2409302, DEB-2337426, DEB-1754518), The Rocky Mountain Biological Laboratory, and Nick Waser & Mary Price.
